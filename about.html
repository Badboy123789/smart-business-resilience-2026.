Who We Are

Engineering at the Heart of What We Do.

Smart Business Resilience, known as SBR, is an engineering-focused company with an interest in the systems and solutions that help buildings and environments function effectively.

Our areas of focus include electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, public health engineering, lighting and sustainability.

We recognise the importance of thoughtful engineering, coordination between disciplines and consideration of long-term performance.
